The Daily LedgerPractice example

Government places a tax on sugary drinks

  1. 1Axes: Price / Quantity
  2. 2D and S₁, equilibrium P₁ Q₁
  3. 3S₁ shifts left to S₂ (gap = tax)
  4. 4New equilibrium P₂ Q₂
  5. 5Shade the burdens and tax revenue
  6. 6Write the answer chain
K1A1A1E1
  1. Tax raises firms' costs → supply shifts left.
  2. Price rises, quantity falls.
  3. The burden depends on PED: with inelastic demand, consumers pay more of it.
  4. Evaluation: if demand is price-inelastic, consumption may fall only a little.

This is how every topic is taught.
